About AVAC Ltd

Turning International Ambitions into Well-Prepared Journeys

AVAC Ltd is a travel, visa application, and international education consultancy based in Kigali – Nyarugenge, Rwanda. We are dedicated to helping individuals, families, students, and professionals achieve their international travel, education, and immigration goals through professional guidance, personalized assistance, and reliable support.

We understand that international travel, studying abroad, and immigration applications can feel complicated and time-consuming. That is why we guide every client step by step—helping you organize documents, understand destination requirements, and navigate each stage of the process with transparency.

  • School admissions guidance for Canada and the United States of America
  • Comprehensive visa assistance for Canada, the USA, the UK, Schengen countries, and Turkey
  • Structured document preparation and tailored one-on-one client support

Our Journey

Growing Alongside Global Ambitions From Kigali

Since opening our doors in 2017, AVAC Ltd has grown alongside the ambitions of Rwandan and regional clients pursuing education, travel, and new chapters abroad.

2017 — Foundation

Built to Simplify International Applications

AVAC Ltd was founded in Kigali with a clear conviction: international education and travel opportunities should be accessible through organized, transparent, and honest guidance rather than guesswork.

2020 – 2023 — Expansion

Growing Into Multi-Destination Admissions & Visas

As client trust deepened, we expanded our expertise across Canadian and U.S. school admissions while building dedicated visa support desks covering the United Kingdom, Schengen countries, and Turkey.

Today — Global Mobility Partner

Kigali’s Trusted Partner for Global Mobility

From our office at TCB House (Town Center Building), 3rd Floor A, Room 13 in Kigali – Nyarugenge, Rwanda, we continue supporting students, families, conference delegates, medical travelers, and Canadian Permanent Residency applicants every day.
