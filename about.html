Who We Are

A Trusted Partner for Students, Families & Professionals

AAVAC Ltd (Admissions Visa Application Center) is a travel, visa application, and international education consultancy headquartered at Town Center Building (TCB), 3rd Floor, Room A13 in Downtown Kigali, Rwanda.

Over the past nine years, we have built a reputation for careful preparation and client-centered guidance. We specialize in school admissions and visa application assistance for clients seeking opportunities in Canada and the United States of America, alongside comprehensive visa application services for the United Kingdom, Schengen countries, and Turkey.

Our goal is straightforward: to make international opportunities more accessible by removing administrative confusion and helping every client submit organized, well-prepared applications.

How We Work

An Organized Four-Step Approach

Every successful application begins with clear expectations and methodical preparation. Here is how our Kigali consultancy guides your file from day one.

01

Individual Assessment

We listen carefully to your travel, academic, or immigration objective and review the specific requirements of your target destination.

02

Document Preparation

We help you gather, organize, structure, and review supporting documents so nothing critical is overlooked before submission.

03

Application Guidance

We guide you through forms, institutional portals, appointment readiness, and procedural steps with clarity and transparency.

04

Ongoing Follow-Up

We stay available throughout the application timeline to assist with next steps, updates, and pre-departure preparation.
